# player-injury-impact-analytics
Analytics innovation project examining player availability, team performance, and post-injury recovery

## Project Overview
This project proposes a player injury impact analysis tool designed to evaluate how player availability affects both individual and team performance in professional soccer. The tool will examine team performance while a player is absent due to injury and compare the player's performance before injury with performance following return. The objective is to provide a more comprehensive view of injury impact by considering both the team's response to lost availability and the player's performance following reintegration.

## Decision-Making Problem
Player injuries create uncertainty for coaching and performance staff because the impact can extend beyond the player's immediate absence. Decision-makers will need to determine whether the team can maintain competitive performance without the player and what level of individual performance can reasonably be expected following return. The tool is intended to reduce this uncertainty by providing evidence that can support tactical adjustments, post-injury performance expectations, and longer-term roster decisions.

## Proposed Analytics Approach
The proposed approach will evaluate performance across three stages of player availability: before injury, during absence, and following return. Individual player ratings before injury will be compared with post-return ratings to identify changes in performance following reintegration. Team match results and goal differential during missed matches will be examined to assess team performance during the player's absence. Injury and return dates will provide additional context for the period of unavailability. These measures will allow player-level and team-level effects to be evaluated separately without assuming that injury alone caused observed performance differences.

## Use by Decision Makers
Coaching and performance staff could use the tool to evaluate how effectively the team adapts when a player is unavailable and whether post-return performance differs from the player's pre-injury benchmark. Rather than relying only on medical clearance or match outcomes, decision-makers could use the performance comparisons as additional context when considering tactical adjustments, player reintegration, and roster planning.

## Connection to Chapter 7
This analytics idea currently aligns with the Creative phase of the Chapter 7 innovation framework. The decision problem and initial analytical approach have been defined to provide a foundation for evaluating the relationship between player availability, team performance, and post-injury performance. In practice, the concept has not yet been tested or refined based on the needs of the coaching and performance staff who would use the information. Progressing into the Prototyping phase would allow the initial approach to be refined through targeted experimentation and stakeholder feedback before broader implementation.

## Prototype Enhancement
The prototype refines the initial analytics approach by incorporating contextual factors that may influence observed performance differences across the injury timeline. Rather than interpreting changes in player ratings or team performance primarily in relation to injury status, the enhancement proposes incorporating length of absence and opponent context. Length of absence can be derived from the injury and return dates, while opponent information can provide additional context for performance across matches. This refinement addresses the potential influence of confounding and hidden factors when interpreting observed performance differences.

Incorporating these factors could improve decision-making by providing additional context for evaluating post-injury performance and the team's response to player absence. This broader context may provide a more meaningful interpretation of observed performance differences and strengthen the analytical value of the tool for decision-makers.
